# Privacy Policy

**Split Flap Display** (the "Extension")
**Effective date:** July 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Split Flap Display browser extension
(the "Extension," "we," or "us") handles information in connection with
your use of the Extension. It is intended to give you a complete and
accurate account of what data the Extension touches, where that data goes,
and the choices available to you.

By installing and using the Extension, you agree to the practices
described in this Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please
do not install or use the Extension.

## 2. Summary

The Extension operates entirely on your local device. We do not operate
any backend server, and we do not collect, receive, transmit to ourselves,
or otherwise have access to any data generated by your use of the
Extension. Where the Extension communicates over the network, it does so
directly between your browser and the specific third-party service
required for the feature in question, and only when you have enabled and,
where applicable, configured that feature yourself.

## 3. Information Handled by Each Feature

The table below summarizes every category of data the Extension may
handle, the purpose for which it is used, its destination, and where (if
anywhere) it is retained.

| Feature | Data Involved | Destination | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weather | Approximate geographic coordinates, obtained via your browser's Geolocation API | Transmitted directly to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) to retrieve current conditions | Coordinates are cached locally in `chrome.storage.sync` to avoid repeated permission prompts |
| Time on Sites *(disabled by default)* | The hostname of the active browser tab (e.g., `github.com`); full URLs, page content, and inactive tabs are never accessed | Not transmitted anywhere; processed and retained exclusively on your device | Stored in `chrome.storage.local`; automatically deleted after three (3) days and reset daily |
| Third-Party Stat Modules — GitHub, Chess.com, WakaTime, Steam, and MonkeyType *(all disabled by default)* | A username or API key that you provide directly | Transmitted directly to the corresponding third-party service's public API | The credential you provide is stored in `chrome.storage.sync` so that it need not be re-entered |
| Crypto Prices *(disabled by default)* | The coin list and currency you configure | Transmitted directly to CoinGecko's public price API | Your coin/currency selection is stored in `chrome.storage.sync` |
| Greeting Holiday Lookup | Today's date and the country you select | Transmitted directly to the public Nager.Date holiday API | Your country selection is stored in `chrome.storage.sync`; holiday data returned is not retained |
| Search Mode | Text you type while Search Mode is active | Transmitted directly to Google Search, or used to navigate directly to a URL if the input resembles one — functionally equivalent to typing into your browser's address bar | Not retained by the Extension |
| Preferences | Your configuration choices (theme, module selection and order, sound settings, and similar preferences) | Not transmitted anywhere | Stored in `chrome.storage.sync`, which synchronizes across your own signed-in browser instances via your Google Account, consistent with standard Chrome extension behavior |

## 4. Browser Permissions

### 4.1 The "tabs" Permission

The optional Time on Sites feature requires the ability to determine which
website is displayed in the active tab in order to measure time spent per
site. This is the sole purpose of the `tabs` permission requested by the
Extension.

This permission has no effect unless you affirmatively enable the Time on
Sites feature in Settings; the feature is disabled by default. Disabling
the feature at any time immediately halts all further tracking. At no
point does the Extension access page content, form data, or tabs other
than the currently active one, regardless of whether this feature is
enabled.

### 4.2 The "alarms" Permission

Used solely to periodically persist and prune data associated with the
Time on Sites feature described above. This permission has no effect if
that feature is disabled.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The Extension communicates with the following third-party services,
solely when the corresponding feature has been enabled and, where
applicable, configured by you:

- **Open-Meteo** — receives approximate coordinates to provide weather
  data; no account or authentication is required.
- **GitHub REST API** — receives the username you provide; no
  authentication is required.
- **Chess.com Public API** — receives the username you provide; no
  authentication is required.
- **MonkeyType Public API** — receives the username you provide; no
  authentication is required.
- **WakaTime API** — receives an API key generated and controlled by you
  through your own WakaTime account.
- **Steam Web API** — receives an API key and Steam ID generated and
  controlled by you through your own Steam account.
- **CoinGecko Public API** — receives the coin list and currency you
  configure; no authentication is required.
- **Nager.Date Public API** — receives the current date and the country
  code you select, to check for a matching public holiday; no
  authentication is required.
- **Google Search** — receives search queries you submit while using
  Search Mode.

Each of these services is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of
service. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the data
practices of these third parties. Our relationship with each service is
limited to the direct API request initiated by your browser as a result of
your configuration.

## 6. Compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

As the Extension does not transmit personal data to any server under our
control, the obligations applicable to a Data Fiduciary under India's
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") are, in most
respects, not engaged, as no such collection or processing occurs on
infrastructure we operate. Nonetheless, in relation to the limited
categories of data the Extension does handle locally (namely, approximate
location and browsing hostnames), the Extension has been designed in
accordance with the DPDP Act's foundational principles:

- **Consent.** The most sensitive optional feature, Time on Sites, is
  disabled by default and requires an explicit, separate opt-in.
- **Purpose Limitation.** Each category of data is used exclusively for
  the specific feature it supports.
- **Data Minimization.** The Time on Sites feature retains hostnames only;
  full URLs and page content are never accessed or stored.
- **Storage Limitation.** Data associated with the Time on Sites feature
  is automatically deleted after three days.
- **Grievance Redressal.** Any concerns regarding this Policy or the
  Extension's data practices may be directed to the contact provided in
  Section 9.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Because no data is retained outside your own device:

- **Access and Deletion.** Uninstalling the Extension immediately removes
  all data stored locally by it. Individual settings may also be cleared
  at any time from the Settings page, or extension storage may be cleared
  directly via `chrome://extensions`.
- **Withdrawal of Consent.** Disabling the Time on Sites feature in
  Settings immediately halts further data collection; any previously
  retained data for that feature is deleted automatically within three
  days regardless.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed toward, and is not knowingly used to collect
information from, children.
